A salon that sounds as good as it looks

Guitars on the walls, a saxophone by the basins, records on the shelf and an old radio on the mantel. Shear Desire is a hair and beauty studio in Aniwatta, Kandy, where the people doing your hair have been at it for over 15 years.

Every visit starts with a conversation. We listen to what you want, tell you honestly what will work for your hair, and take our time getting it right.
